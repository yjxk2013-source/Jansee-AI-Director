# JANSEE V4 Product Contract

## Core promise

The user provides rough source material and a simple creative intention. JANSEE's AI Director makes the production decisions and returns a complete, polished short video.

## The three creation modes

| Mode | Input | Expected output |
|---|---|---|
| 图片成片 | Product / scene images | Complete short video |
| 视频配音 | One source video | Video with director-led voiceover, subtitles, music and finishing |
| 多视频分镜合成 | Multiple source videos | A coherent, paced, complete short video |

## Interaction principles

- One obvious starting point; plain-language prompts, not editing terminology.
- The user supplies source facts and creative intent. The Director owns the production plan.
- No multi-track timeline, keyframe editor, or professional editing workspace.
- Expose advanced controls only where they materially improve the output.
- All modes and task operations remain available on PC and APP.
- Clearly distinguish supported production behavior from future or preview behavior.

## Quality and trust

- Do not obscure or materially alter the user's product identity.
- Output must be checked for duration, audio/video integrity, subtitles, compliance markings, and visual defects.
- Clearly state output retention and deletion behavior; metadata deletion must match physical object deletion.
- Failed paid jobs must be refunded exactly once through an auditable ledger.
